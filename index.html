1 · Tell the platform your column names (optional)

Your Excel files can keep their own headings. For each column, list the other names it goes by, separated by commas. The platform reads every file using these names plus the standard ones. Leave a box blank if the standard name is enough. Changes re-read any files already loaded.

Are Plant, Cold Rolling Mill and Steel Plant Centre written in one column?
Entries like Plant 1-CRM-1-SPC North are split at the hyphens into the three parts. Statements like “Plant: X, CRM: Y, SPC: Z” are read too.
Does every Excel have an Average Monthly Demand column?
If blank, the first month-like demand column is used.

How the platform reads your Excel

It looks for these columns, under the standard names or the names you list in step 1:

Customer Grade Thickness (mm) Width (mm) Length (mm) TDC Average Monthly Demand (MT) Plant Cold Rolling Mill Steel Plant Centre

  • Column order and position do not matter. Title rows above the table are ignored.
  • Plant, Cold Rolling Mill and Steel Plant Centre can be three columns or one column split by hyphens.
  • Length (mm) holds a number for cut-to-length, or the word Coil.
  • Month-wise demand columns are ignored. If there is no average column, one month’s demand is used and clearly flagged.
  • Grade and TDC must match exactly (capitals, spaces and dashes are ignored).
  • If a heading is not recognised, AI (when on) suggests the match. Confident matches are applied and flagged “AI-matched”; others wait for your confirmation.
  • If something still cannot be found, the platform says what is missing and lets you pick the column from a list.

How the platform reads your Excel

It needs one table with these columns:

Customer Grade Thickness (mm) Width (mm) TDC Average Monthly Demand (MT) Plant Cold Rolling Mill Steel Plant Centre

  • Column order and position do not matter. It reads each column by its heading.
  • Title rows or notes above the table are ignored. The platform finds the heading row itself.
  • Month-wise demand columns are ignored. Only the average monthly demand column is used.
  • Grade and TDC must match exactly (capitals, spaces and dashes are ignored).
  • If a heading is named differently, the platform shows what it could not find and lets you pick the right column from a list.
